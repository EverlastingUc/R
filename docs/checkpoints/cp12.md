# Checkpoint 12 — Activation Functions

## Objective

Implement sigmoid, tanh, ReLU, leaky ReLU, and stable softmax, and verify their
outputs, ranges, and derivatives.

## What was built

`src/nn/activations.py` implements the five requested activation functions
plus identity. Elementwise derivatives are provided for identity, sigmoid,
tanh, ReLU, and leaky ReLU. Softmax has a separate Jacobian function because
each output depends on every logit in its row. It reuses the stable softmax
from Checkpoint 09.

## Evidence

- Hand-calculated outputs and ranges are covered in `tests/test_activations.py`.
- Elementwise derivatives and the softmax Jacobian match centered finite
  differences within `rtol=1e-6`, `atol=1e-8`.
- Focused tests: **13 passed**. Full suite: **92 passed**.
- `black .` and `ruff check .` passed.

## Limitations

At the ReLU kink (`x = 0`), the derivative is defined as zero. The leaky-ReLU
derivative at zero is defined as one. These are documented implementation
choices for points where a classical derivative is not unique or not defined.
