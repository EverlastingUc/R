# Checkpoint 02 — Biological and Artificial Neurons

## Objective

Explain the idea of a biological neuron at a conceptual level and implement a
mathematical artificial neuron.

## What was built

`src/nn/neuron.py` implements one artificial neuron. It multiplies each input
by its corresponding weight, adds a bias, then applies either an identity or a
sigmoid activation:

```text
z = sum(input × weight) + bias
output = activation(z)
```

The implementation checks vector lengths, dimensions, and finite values. Its
sigmoid calculation avoids overflow for large positive and negative inputs.

The biological comparison is conceptual: a biological neuron receives signals
through connections and may send signals onward; an artificial neuron is a
small mathematical calculation inspired by that simplified picture. The two
are not biologically equivalent.
