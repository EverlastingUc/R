# Checkpoint 11 — Artificial Neuron

## Objective

Calculate weighted inputs, add bias, and apply an activation, with at least 20
verified cases.

## What was built

`src/nn/neuron.py` implements a single artificial neuron with identity and
sigmoid activation. It computes a weighted sum and applies the selected
activation, while checking input dimensions and finite values.

## Evidence

- The existing `tests/test_neuron.py` provides **32** hand-calculated and
  validation cases, exceeding the 20-case acceptance line.
- Focused tests passed: 32 passed (recorded in Checkpoint 02).
- The full suite after Phase 03 Checkpoint 12 passed: **92 passed**.

## Limitations

This is one neuron; the next checkpoints connect neurons into layers and a
multilayer network.

## Next step
